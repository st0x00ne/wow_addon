# Forever Wayfinder

A quest companion and personal Discovery Journal for **World of Warcraft: Forever**.

## Features

- **Discovery Journal:** Records quests, explored zones, dungeon visits, and level milestones as you play.
- **Atlas:** Explore illustrated zone overviews, browse Classic and Forever quest checklists, and track class quests in their own chapter.
- **Search and filters:** Find entries by zone, type, class, character, and more.
- **Personal notes:** Add notes and tags, favorite entries, and mark places to revisit.
- **Classic quest chains:** View connected quests, objectives, quest givers, requirements, reference rewards, and map waypoints.
- **Where next?:** Find important class quests, standout reward chains, and Classic quest starts for your level and progress.
- **Quest markers:** `∞` marks possible Forever quests missing from the Classic reference. `D` marks dungeon quests.
- **Reading options:** Choose Standard, Large, or Extra Large text.

## Class priorities and Special quests

**Where next?** has four categories:

- **All:** Important class abilities first, then special reward chains, other class quests, and zone suggestions.
- **Class:** Class abilities and training, including forms, hunter pets, totems, stances, and poisons. Includes 35 curated Classic ability and training routes across all nine Classic classes.
- **Special:** 15 curated reward chains, including Whirlwind weapons, Verigan's Fist, and Carrot on a Stick. See the reward items, hover for item tooltips, and find your next available or active quest step.
- **Zones:** Browse suggested Classic quest starts by zone.

Suggestions check your character's requirements and quest progress. Completed routes drop out, and unfinished class ability routes can remain visible after you've outleveled the usual zone quests. Click a starter waypoint or open a quest already in your log.

These routes and rewards are **Classic references**. Check your live Forever quest log for the actual quest requirements and rewards.

## Getting started

Click the **minimap compass** or the **Journal** button in your quest log.

Open **Atlas** from the Discovery Journal, or use `/fw atlas`.
Start in **Zones** for a zone overview, open **Quests** for its Classic or Forever
checklist, or choose **Class quests** for your class chapter. Use **Show unfinished**
to focus the checklist and **Text** to change reading size. Drag an empty area to
move the Atlas; its position is saved.

The quest log also includes **Chain** and **Where next?** buttons.

Slash commands:

- `/fw journal` — Open the journal.
- `/fw atlas` — Open the Atlas.
- `/fw` — Open Where next?
- `/fw chain` — Show a supported selected quest’s chain.

## Notes

Works standalone; Questie is not required.

The journal is saved locally across your account, with filters for individual characters. It records your journey from installation onward.

Classic quest information is reference data. Locations, requirements, chains, and rewards may differ on Forever. The `∞` marker identifies a possible Forever quest, not a confirmed one.

Atlas totals cover mapped, one-time Classic zone quests in its reference, filtered for your current character. Class quests have their own section; repeatable, event, and specially gated class entries are visible but unscored. Click **To find** to open a Classic quest's starter map and set a waypoint; this replaces your current waypoint. **Map only** means the reference has no reliable starter coordinate. Forever may move Classic quest starters. The Atlas records Forever quests as you encounter them. These totals are a growing reference, not a guarantee that every live Forever quest is cataloged.
